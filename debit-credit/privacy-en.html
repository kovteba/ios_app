Privacy Policy

Debit Credit, a personal budgeting app

Українська версія

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Debit Credit does not collect any personal data. The developer receives neither your financial records nor any information about how you use the app.

Where your data lives

Accounts, transactions, categories, budgets and settings are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, the same data is also stored in your private iCloud (the CloudKit private database) under your Apple account. The developer cannot access it.

Receipt photos

Receipt photos are stored only if you keep "Save receipt photos" turned on. If it is off, no photo is kept after a receipt is scanned. Saved photos live alongside the rest of your data: on your device and, with iCloud sync on, in your private iCloud.

Network requests

The only network requests the app makes are exchange-rate requests to the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua). They carry no personal data.

Apple Pay

Apple Pay payment details come only from your own Shortcuts automation and stay on your device.

Analytics, ads and third parties

The app has no analytics, ads, tracking, accounts or third-party SDKs. Your data is not shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Data in iCloud can be removed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Any changes are posted on this page together with the date.
