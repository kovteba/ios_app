## Name

## Promotional Text
Apple Pay purchases log themselves, receipts sort into categories item by item, and budgets warn you before you overspend. Your data stays on your device.

## Description
Debit Credit keeps track of your money with as little typing as possible: expenses land in the right category, budgets keep an eye on your limits, and reports show where your money goes.

APPLE PAY LOGS EXPENSES FOR YOU
Set up an automation in Shortcuts once, and every Apple Pay payment adds its amount and payee to the app. Payee rules fill in the right category for you.

RECEIPT SCANNING
Scan a receipt with the camera or add it from Photos or Files. It is recognised on your device, and every item gets its own category, so one supermarket trip splits into groceries, pet supplies and the rest without manual entry.

CATEGORIES AND SUBCATEGORIES
Start with a ready-made set and make it yours: add subcategories, rename, pick icons and colours. Split a single transaction across several categories.

ACCOUNTS IN ANY CURRENCY
Cards, cash and other accounts in hryvnias, dollars, euros and other currencies. Exchange rates come from the National Bank of Ukraine, or you can set them by hand. Your total balance is shown in your main currency.

TRANSFERS
Move money between your own accounts, including currency exchange, without distorting your income or expenses.

BUDGETS THAT WARN YOU IN TIME
Set limits per week, month, year or a custom period, for all spending or for single categories. You get a notification at 80% and at 100% of the limit, so overspending never comes as a surprise.

REPORTS AND TRENDS
Charts by category, income against expenses and month-by-month trends show what costs you most and how your habits change.

PRIVACY
The app collects no data: no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your records are stored on your device, and the only network requests fetch exchange rates from the NBU, with no personal data attached.

Available in English and Ukrainian, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
