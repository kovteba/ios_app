Debit Credit

Budget, expenses, receipts. Personal budgeting with no accounts and no tracking.

Особистий бюджет без облікових записів і без стеження.

Debit Credit keeps track of your money with as little typing as possible: expenses land in the right category, budgets keep an eye on your limits, and reports show where your money goes.

Features

  • Apple Pay logs expenses for youA one-time Shortcuts automation adds every Apple Pay payment with its amount and payee. Payee rules pick the category.
  • Receipt scanningScan with the camera or add from Photos or Files. Recognised on device; every item gets its own category.
  • Categories and subcategoriesA ready-made set you can rename, recolour and extend. Split one transaction across several categories.
  • Accounts in any currencyCards, cash and other accounts in UAH, USD, EUR and more. Rates from the National Bank of Ukraine or set by hand.
  • TransfersMove money between your own accounts, including currency exchange, without distorting income or expenses.
  • Budgets that warn you in timeLimits per week, month, year or a custom period. Notifications at 80% and 100%.
  • Reports and trendsCharts by category, income against expenses, and month-by-month trends.
  • PrivacyNo ads, no analytics, no tracking. Data stays on your device and, optionally, in your private iCloud.
